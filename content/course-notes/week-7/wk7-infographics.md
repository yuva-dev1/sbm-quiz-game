Srimad Bhagavatam Certification Course — Session 7: Canto 1, Chapters 7-17

Chapter 7:
- Story of Parikshith — prelude to Krishna Katha.
- Srimad Bhagavatam begins where Mahabharata ends.
- Ashwatthama seeks to avenge the death of Dronacharya by killing Pandava's 5 sons.
- Arjuna captured and spared Ashwatthama's life in an attempt to follow Bhagavan's request: "Do that which would please all the parties and Me!"

Chapter 8:
- Lord Krishna protected the child in Uttara's womb and all the Pandavas from Ashwatthama's Bhramastra.
- Queen Kunti extolled Bhagavan (Kunti Stuti) and prayed that her mind dwell constantly in Bhagavan.

Chapters 9-11:
- Bhishmacharya extolled Bhagavan (Bhishma Stuti) and prayed for the love that the Gopis had for Lord Krishna and attained moksham.
- Bhagavan leaves Hastinapuram and returns to Dwaraka.

Chapter 12:
- Parikshith's Horoscope: future king → cursed to die in 7 days → attains Lord Krishna's feet.
- Named Vishnuraatha (saved/given by Vishnu) & Parikshith (seeker of that divine vision).
- Krishna attends Yudhishthira's Ashwamedha at Hastinapuram, then goes to Dwaraka with Arjuna.

Chapter 13:
- Dhritarashtra, Gandhari & Vidura left for Saptasrota (Himalayan ashram).
- Narada consoles grieving Yudhishthira, reveals that Dhritarashtra & Gandhari quit their mortal coil there; Vidura resumed pilgrimage.

Chapters 14-15:
- Arjuna realizes his prowess was Bhagavan's grace.
- Kunti Devi, Pandavas, and Vidura left their mortal coil.
- Parikshith became the king.

Chapters 16-17:

4 legs of Dharma:
1. Tapas (Austerity)
2. Shaucham (Purity)
3. Daya (Compassion)
4. Satyam (Truth)

- Parikshith provided Kali Purusha 5 places to reside: gambling, alcohol, adultery, violence, and gold.
- Parikshith did not kill Kali Purusha because everyone could attain.
- Bhagavan through Namasankirtanam in Kali Yuga.
- Good intentions are rewarded; bad intentions are not punished unless executed upon.
